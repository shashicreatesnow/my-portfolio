# Master App Thumbnails

> Improving thumbnail CTR through craft on Eloelo's Master app — median CTR doubled, miss rate halved, across 833 live thumbnails over 4.5 months.

## Quick Facts

- **Client:** Eloelo (Master app — high-volume vernacular short-form content platform)
- **Category:** Visual Design
- **Role:** Thumbnail Designer
- **Timeline:** 8 Dec 2025 → 22 Apr 2026 (~4.5 months · 833 thumbnails)
- **Industry:** Creator Economy / Vernacular Content / Hindi-first
- **Status:** Live / Ongoing

## The Problem

The Master app is a Hindi-first, scroll-feed content platform. As thumbnail designer, I produce 50+ thumbnails a week across 16+ categories — Devotion, Finance, Sports, Astrology, Trending, Tech, Govt schemes — under daily deadlines.

When I pulled my own data in early 2026, the picture was honest: median CTR 0.81%, with **56.5% of my thumbnails clicking under 1%**. Five months of plateau. I had taste; I didn't have a craft. The task wasn't to make a great thumbnail — it was to stop making weak ones.

## The Approach

A four-shift transformation across **Text → Layout → Colour → AI**, surfaced once I started looking at my own work the way I'd look at someone else's. The shifts didn't happen on one day; they emerged decision by decision.

### The Turn (late March 2026)
Was iterating fast with AI for variations. One iteration came back and I stopped — *this isn't a fluke, I can name what's working, I can do it on purpose.* That was the turn. Not a tool. A way of looking. From there I built a system around the four shifts and the AI workflow.

## The Solution — The Four Shifts

### Shift 01 — Text: from decoration to delivery
- **Principle:** One headline, said well, beats six things shouted at once.
- Cut elements first. Stylised type built in Illustrator (or shaped with AI), tuned for contrast, sized for legibility on a 4-inch phone. Text became part of the composition, not pasted on top.

### Shift 02 — Layout: from arranging to directing
- **Principle:** One subject in the middle. Everything else points at it.
- Single focal point. Peripheral elements give just enough context. Learnt to call when graphic vs headline should lead the hierarchy.
- **Craft tactic:** desaturate the image to test contrast. If it reads in greyscale, it works in the feed.

### Shift 03 — Colour: from palette to hierarchy
- **Principle:** Pick the colour that already belongs to the topic, then build around it.
- 3-step hierarchy: (1) Context first — recognisable colour for the topic. (2) Psychology second — what should they feel before they read? (3) Text last — contrast, not defaulted to white.

### Shift 04 — AI: from button to system
- **Principle:** AI is a junior designer that produces fifty drafts. The taste is still mine.
- Custom AI agents researching reference styles per topic + reusable prompt templates + a library of predefined text styles for the agents to work from. Bridges to my Gamma case study.

## The Outcome

| Metric | Dec 2025 | April 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Median CTR | 0.81% | **1.52%** |
| Miss rate (CTR < 1%) | 56.5% | **23.3%** |
| Hit rate (CTR ≥ 3%) | 8.7% | **14.0%** |
| Total impressions | 3.0M | 17.1M |

**The fortnight inflection:** 09–22 Mar median 0.47% → 23 Mar–05 Apr median 1.08% → 06–19 Apr median 1.50%. Held through April.

**Category-level lift:** every major category moved up. AI Se Editing — historically weakest — went from 0.40% (Mar) to 1.99% (Apr). Not luck; not category-specific.
